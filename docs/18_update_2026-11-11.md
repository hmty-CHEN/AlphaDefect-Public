## What carried over between characters

The engine, the network, the training system and most of the evaluation protocol are shared; a character is selected by configuration (`character` in the training config). The Regent started from the Defect setup, and the Silent started from the Regent's configuration with the early-training settings (32,768 steps per batch, the Regent's starting curriculum; Silent DR #1). Several Defect practices were applied to the new lines directly: segmented run submission, lowering the learning rate while enlarging the batch once progress flattens, and moving practice share from fights the agent already wins to bosses. Two were re-tested on the Regent:

- **EMA weights.** On the Defect a single EMA network matched the raw weights (DR #265); on the Regent the EMA copy of v53,840 beat the raw weights of the same version by 4.4 pp on 1,000 paired W1 seeds (72.2% vs 67.8%, p = 0.013), level with the 8-snapshot ensemble (addendum to Regent DR #24). On the Silent the EMA copy of v24,721 matched the raw weights (66.2% vs 65.6% on 1,000 paired W1 seeds, p = 0.78; Silent DR #15).
- **Snapshot ensembles.** On the Regent, 8 snapshots about 1,000 versions apart beat the single snapshot v53,840 by 5.2 pp (73.0% vs 67.8%, p = 0.003), while 4 snapshots about 500 versions apart gained 1.9 pp (p = 0.29), the same pattern as on the Defect (DR #272). On the Silent, 8 snapshots spread over its last ~1,360 versions gained nothing (65.6% vs 65.6%), although they disagreed on more decisions than the Regent's E8 (21.0% vs 19.0% of top choices; Silent DR #15).

Each line keeps its own decision log so that the numbering and the guardrails of one line do not leak into another: Regent decisions are cited as "Regent DR #n", Silent decisions as "Silent DR #n", and the main log keeps "DR #n".

## Open questions added by this update

- Would skipping easy fights help while the learning curve is still rising? On a flat curve it added runs (1.33× per hour) but no measurable strength over 500 versions ([21](21_combat_outcome_prediction_and_skipping.md)).
- Why did EMA weights and snapshot ensembles help the Regent (+4.4 and +5.2 pp) but not the Silent (+0.6 and +0.0 pp) or the Defect? Act-3 search helped all three (+3.1 to +4.9 pp).
- Ironclad and Necrobinder have observations but no training yet.
